Suppressor Bench

Every can from the 2025 and 2026 TBAC Silencer Summits, with price, weight, length past the muzzle and sound side by side. Sound is only comparable on the same host and bore, so pick one, set what matters to you, and star the cans you want to keep, with a note on why.

What matters to you

Each can gets 0–100 per measure against the other cans in the same host and bore (100 is the quietest, lightest, shortest or cheapest). The score is the weighted average. A can missing a measure you weight (usually price) gets no score.

Results

Click a column to sort. Click any price or length to enter your own number; it is saved in this browser and shown in blue.

Starred cans

Stars, notes, weights and your own numbers stay in this browser. The share link carries your stars and notes; copy a backup to move everything to another device.

Sound is the mil-spec average dB(A) of the left and right muzzle mics (lower is quieter). Ear is dB(A) at the shooter's ear. Data: TBAC Silencer Summit 2025 and 2026; both years used the same hosts and mics, so they can be pooled.

Length past muzzle is the Summit overall length, except for over-the-barrel cans (tagged OTB), where it is the length added to the gun: Dead Air Nomad Ti OTB 4.6 in (Dead Air), Unknown OG 30 4.0 in (Unknown Suppressors), B&T TiRe-X with OTB about 4.1 in (estimate from reviews). TiRe-X Compact has no published figure, so it shows overall length with a question mark until you enter one.

Price comes from Kevin's price list first (Oct 2026, before tax and stamp), then the whichcan.com catalog MSRP (tagged cat.). Orange prices are estimates. One very high price in a field, like $6,000 on the Typhon 56, squeezes everyone else's price scores together.
